load_usda_catalog() returns owned copies of compatible five-field foods plus
categories/limits/equivalences and separate metadata. metadata_by_id(current_foods)
must receive the current catalog after SQLite/custom changes. Only exact matches
of all four nutrient amounts retain USDA source attribution. Modified/malformed
rows get source=None, provenance_status=customized, auto_generation=False. Meal
slots are editorial, so changing only them does not claim they were USDA data.
No network calls, database writes or database imports occur in this module.
